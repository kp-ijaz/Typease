# Customer guide

For people who use a typing centre's online portal: individual customers, and the administrators and
employees of companies the centre serves.

The portal belongs to your typing centre and may carry its name, logo and colours. The examples below use
the invented centre "Al Noor Typing Centre" from the demo data. Buttons and menu names are written exactly as
they appear on screen in English; the portal is also available in Arabic.

> **What the portal is, and is not.** The portal lets you ask the centre for a service, send documents, approve
> a price, pay and follow progress. The centre's staff do the work. The portal is not a government website
> and is not connected to any government system. When an authority decides on an application, the centre can
> only tell you what its staff or its partner reported, and the portal says so next to every such status.

## 1. Getting to the portal

Use the link your centre gave you. It is one of these:

- the centre's own web address, if it has one;
- an address that starts with the centre's short name;
- the shared portal address followed by `?tenant=` and the centre's short name.

If you see **We could not find this centre**, the address is incomplete. Open the link from the centre
again.

In the page header (on a phone: in the menu) you can change the **Language** (English or Arabic) and the
**Appearance** (Light, Dark, or Match device).

![Home page of a centre's portal](../brand/screenshots/customer-home-whitelabel-en-light-desktop.png)

## 2. Things you can do without an account

### How do I see what the centre offers?

Open **Services**. Search by name or choose a category. Each service shows:

- **Documents you need**, marked **Required** or **If available**;
- **Fees (estimate)**: **Centre service fee**, **Government fees**, **Third-party fees**, **VAT** and an
  **Estimated total**. These are estimates. The amount you pay is the one in your quotation;
- **Usual processing time**;
- a sentence saying who submits the application. For many services the centre prepares your documents and
  **you submit them to the authority yourself**. Read this sentence before you start.

Some services show **At the centre** instead of **Available online**: visit or call the centre for those.

If the page says the service list is not published online, the centre has chosen not to show it. Contact the
centre.

### How do I check a request without signing in?

Open **Track request**. Enter the **Request number** (for example `TE-000123`) and the **Tracking code** you
received when the request was submitted (ten letters and numbers, for example `K7MQP-X2RTD`), then **Check
status**.

You see the service, the status and the progress so far. You do not see documents, messages or amounts; sign
in for those. The tracking code is shown only once, when the request is submitted. If you lost it, sign in or
ask the centre.

## 3. Creating an account and signing in

### How do I create an account?

1. Choose **Sign up**.
2. Enter your **Full name**, **Email address**, **Mobile number** and a **Password** (at least 10 characters,
   with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a number; the centre's installation may require more
   characters, and the form says so).
3. Tick **I accept the terms of service and the privacy notice.** You can read both first. Ticking **Send me
   offers and news from the centre** is optional.
4. Choose **Create account**. The centre sends a confirmation link to your email address. Open it within 48
   hours. Until you do, you cannot sign in.

If the sign-up page says **This centre creates accounts for its customers**, ask the centre to invite you.
You then receive an email with a link to choose your password.

### How do I sign in?

Choose **Log in**, enter your email address and password, then **Sign in**.

- **Forgot your password?** sends a link to choose a new one. It is valid for one hour. Choosing a new
  password signs you out on all devices.
- **Sign in with a code sent to your phone** sends a 6-digit code by SMS to your verified mobile number. This
  works only if the centre has SMS set up.
- After five wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes.
- If you switched on two-step verification, you are also asked for the code from your authenticator app.

### What is on the dashboard?

**Waiting for you** lists requests that need something from you: a draft to finish, a document to replace, a
quotation to approve, an invoice to pay. **In progress** lists the rest. Each line says what happens next.

## 4. Starting a request

Choose **New request**, or **Start this request** on a service page. There are four steps: **Service**,
**Details**, **Documents**, **Review**.

1. **Service.** Choose the service and, if the centre has several, the **Branch** that will handle your
   request and where you collect documents. Then **Start this request**. Nothing is sent to the centre yet.
2. **Details.** Fill in what the centre asks for. Fields marked `*` are required. Some fields are already
   filled in **From your profile – please check**. You can add a note under **Anything else the centre should
   know?**
3. **Documents.** Upload each document on the list. A clear photo from your phone is enough; the whole page
   must be visible and readable. Accepted files are PDF, JPG, PNG and WebP. If you already have a valid copy
   on your file, the portal offers **Use this one**. You can continue with a required document missing and
   add it later, but the centre can only start once it has it.
4. **Review.** Check your details and documents, then **Submit request**. By submitting you confirm that the
   details and documents are correct and yours to use.

Everything you enter is saved as a draft with its own number. Use **Save and finish later** and continue on
any device from **My requests**. **Discard this draft** cancels it for good.

After you submit you see the **request number** and the **tracking code**. **Keep the tracking code: it is
shown only this once.**

What happens next is shown on the same page. In general:

1. The centre checks your details and documents and tells you exactly what is missing or unclear.
2. You receive a quotation with the exact amount. Nothing is charged before you approve it.
3. You pay, before the work starts or when it is completed, depending on the service.
4. The centre prepares your documents. Depending on the service it hands them to you to submit yourself,
   passes them to an authorised partner, or submits them through the channels it is authorised to use.

## 5. Your documents

### How do I upload a document for a request?

Open the request, go to the **Documents** tab and choose **Upload** on the line. Files are checked for viruses
and stored encrypted. While you see **Security check in progress**, wait a moment. If a file was blocked by
the security check it was not saved: upload another copy.

### The centre could not accept my document. What now?

The line shows **The centre could not accept this document** with the reason the centre wrote. Choose
**Upload a new copy**. Then send the request back to the centre (section 6).

A document the centre has already accepted cannot be changed online.

### How do I keep documents on file for next time?

Open **My documents** and choose **Upload a document**. Say what kind of document it is and, if it has one,
its **Expiry date** as printed on the document. The centre confirms the date when it checks the document.

If you allow it (section 15, **Reusing your documents**), valid documents on your file are attached to new
requests automatically. Otherwise the portal offers them and you choose.

To replace a document with a newer one, choose **Upload a new version**. The new file takes over once it has
passed the security check, and the centre checks it again.

### How do I download a document?

Open it from **My documents** or from the request. Download links work for a few minutes only, and every
access is recorded. Documents marked **From the centre** are those the centre completed for you.

### What does "Expiring soon" mean?

The list shows documents whose expiry date, according to the centre's records, is near or has passed. **Request
renewal** starts a new request for it. Check the document itself for the exact date; the centre's records are
only as good as what was entered.

## 6. When the centre needs something from you

The request shows **Action needed from you**, and you get a notification.

1. Open the request. **The centre wrote:** tells you what is needed.
2. Do it: upload the missing or replacement documents (**Go to my documents**), or correct your details on
   the **Details** tab (**Edit my details**).
3. Choose **Send back to the centre**. Work continues from where it stopped.

You cannot send the request back while a document that was not accepted has not been replaced. Nothing moves
on the centre's side until you send it back.

## 7. Quotations: approving the price

When the centre has checked your request, you receive a quotation. Open it from **Quotations** or from the
request (**Review the quotation**).

The quotation lists **Centre service fees**, **Government fees** (collected on behalf of the relevant
authority), **Third-party charges**, any **Discount**, **VAT** and the **Total**, and is **valid until** the
date shown. **Download PDF** gives you a copy.

- **Approve quotation**: you accept the total. Nothing is charged at that moment. An invoice is created that
  you can pay online or at the centre.
- **Decline quotation**: give a reason, so that the centre can send a new quotation or contact you.

An expired quotation cannot be approved; the centre sends a new one. If the centre replaced or withdrew a
quotation, the page says so.

## 8. Paying

Open **Payments**. The **To pay** tab shows your outstanding balance and the invoices to pay. **Invoices**
lists all of them; **Payments and receipts** lists what you paid.

### How do I pay online?

Open the invoice and choose **Pay online**. You are taken to the page of the centre's payment provider and
enter your card there. The portal never sees your card number. Afterwards you return to the portal:

| You see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Payment received** | The provider confirmed the payment. Your receipt is ready |
| **Confirming your payment…** | The portal is waiting for the provider's confirmation. Keep the page open |
| **We have no confirmation yet** | The confirmation can take a few minutes. Choose **Check again**. Do not pay twice |
| **The payment did not go through** | No money was taken. Try again from the invoice |
| **The payment was not completed** | You left the payment page before paying. Nothing was charged |

If you see **Online payment is not available**, the centre does not take online payments. Pay at the centre.

A page titled **Test payment** means the centre is using a test provider: no money is taken and no card is
needed. You should only see this on a demonstration system.

### How do I pay at the centre?

Pay by cash, card or bank transfer at the centre and mention the invoice number. The centre records the
payment and it appears under **Payments and receipts**.

### Where is my receipt?

Under **Payments and receipts**, choose **Download receipt**. Invoices and receipts are PDF files in English.

## 9. Following your request

Open the request from **My requests**. The **Overview** tab shows the status, who it is waiting for, and
**What happens next**.

| Status you see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft — not submitted yet | You have not submitted the request |
| Submitted | The centre has received it |
| Documents under review | The centre is checking your details and documents |
| Action needed from you | The centre asked you for something (section 6) |
| Quotation ready for approval | Review and approve the price (section 7) |
| Payment required | Pay so that the centre can start (section 8) |
| Queued for processing | Waiting for a member of staff to start |
| Being prepared | Staff are preparing your documents |
| Ready for submission | Prepared, about to be submitted |
| Submitted — in processing | The centre recorded that the application was submitted |
| Returned for correction | The authority or partner sent it back; the centre is working on it |
| Application was not accepted | The centre will contact you about what to do next |
| Final checks | Last check before your documents are ready |
| Completed | Done |
| Cancelled | Stopped |

**Expected by** is an estimate from the centre. Authorities decide their own timing.

### What is "Status at the authority"?

For applications submitted outside the centre, the request shows the authority, the reference number, how it
was submitted and a status. **Every such status says where it comes from**, for example "Entered by staff
(not verified by the authority)" or "Provided by partner". It is what the centre's staff or its partner
reported. It is not an official confirmation. For anything that matters, check with the authority using the
reference number.

## 10. Messages and support

- **About one request:** open the request and use the **Messages** tab. Write your question and choose **Send
  message**. The centre answers during its working hours and you get a notification. Answers from staff show
  the staff member's first name only. **Messages** in the menu lists all these conversations.
- **About anything else:** open **Support** and choose **Ask a question**. Give a subject, a topic (request
  status, documents, payment, appointment, account, other), your question and, if useful, files. You see the
  answer in the same place. Replying to a conversation the centre marked as resolved reopens it.

A closed request no longer takes messages; use **Support** instead.

## 11. Appointments

Open **Appointments** and choose **Book an appointment**. Pick the **Branch**, the **Day**, what the visit is
for and one of the free times, then **Book**. If the branch is closed or full on that day, choose another
day.

You can **Reschedule** or **Cancel appointment** from the list, as long as it is not too close to the
appointment time; after that, call the centre. If the centre sends reminders, you receive one before the
appointment.

## 12. Notifications

Open **Notifications**. The **Inbox** tab lists updates about your requests, payments and appointments.
**How we contact you** lets you switch channels on and off: **Email**, **Text message (SMS)**, **WhatsApp**,
**Mobile app notifications** and **Notifications in this portal**.

- Messages that protect your account (confirmation links, password reset, one-time codes) are always sent.
- Offers and WhatsApp messages also need your permission (section 15).
- Which channels actually work depends on what the centre has set up.

The centre never sends your documents by email. You open them in the portal.

## 13. When your request is complete

You get a notification, and the request shows **Completed**. **Your completed documents** on the request are
the documents the centre prepared for you: download them there, or collect paper documents at the branch as
the centre tells you.

Under **How did we do?** you can give a rating from one to five stars and a comment, once per request.

## 14. Cancelling a request

Open the request and choose **Cancel request**. Give a reason; the centre sees it. Cancelling cannot be
undone.

- You can cancel online until the centre starts working: while the request is a draft, submitted, under
  review, waiting for you, waiting for your approval or waiting for payment.
- After that the portal says **The centre has already started working on this request**. Write to the centre
  in the **Messages** tab.
- **Cancelling does not refund by itself.** If you already paid, the centre reviews what can be returned and
  contacts you.

## 15. Your profile, security and privacy

Open **Profile**. It has three tabs.

### My details

You can change your **Full name**, **Name in Arabic**, **Language** and **Address**. Your mobile number,
nationality, date of birth, Emirates ID and passport are shown under **On file with the centre** and only the
centre can change them, because they are checked against your documents. Identity numbers are shown with only
their last digits. If one is wrong, choose **Something is wrong? Ask for a correction**.

### Password and security

Change your password; switch on **Two-step verification** with an authenticator app (you receive recovery
codes: save them, they are shown only once); and see **Devices signed in**, where you can sign out a device
you do not recognise.

### Privacy

**What the centre may use your data for** lists six permissions. You can switch each one off at any time,
except the first:

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| Handling your requests | Using your details and documents for the services you request. Needed; it cannot be switched off here |
| Expiry reminders | A reminder before your passport, Emirates ID or residence expires |
| Offers and news | Occasional offers and news from the centre |
| Automatic reading of documents | Software reads your documents to pre-fill forms. Staff always check the result. If you have never chosen, the centre may use it; switch it off here to stop it |
| Reusing your documents | Valid documents on your file are attached to new requests |
| WhatsApp messages | Updates about your requests on WhatsApp |

**Your data** lets you ask the centre for:

| Request | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A copy of my data / Access to my data | The centre prepares a file with your profile, requests, list of documents, invoices and payments. **Download my data** appears here when it is ready. Keep the file safe: it contains your identity numbers |
| Correct my data | Say what is wrong and what is correct. The centre checks your documents and corrects your file |
| Delete my data | The centre reviews the request and tells you what will be deleted and what it must keep. Deletion cannot be undone: your account stops working and your documents are removed. Invoices and payments are kept. It is not possible while a request is open or an invoice is unpaid |

The page shows the date by which the centre plans to answer (30 days by default) and the centre's answer.
How your data is used is described in the centre's **Privacy policy**, linked at the bottom of every page.

## 16. For companies: the corporate portal

If your company is a client of the centre, the centre invites one or more people from your company to the
**corporate portal**. You cannot register a company yourself: open the link in the invitation email, choose a
password and sign in.

![Overview page of the corporate portal](../brand/screenshots/corporate-overview-en-light-desktop.png)

**Signing in and your account.** Signing in, forgotten password, accepting an invitation, your own
**Profile** and **Security** (password, two-step verification, signed-in devices).

**Overview**: your account number and status; counts of employees, open requests, requests waiting for your
company and unpaid invoices; **Requests by stage**; the latest requests; your company's trade licence and
tax registration number as the centre holds them; and your **Payment terms** (credit limit, available credit
and payment due date, or "pays each invoice when it is issued").

If the overview says **Your company is being verified**, the centre is checking your trade licence. You can
read, but employees and requests can be added once the account is verified. **This account is suspended**
means new requests, employees and invitations are paused; contact the centre.

**The menu**, for a company administrator:

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Employees** | Your employee list with documents and expiry dates. **Add employee** (only the name is required), **Edit employee**, **Deactivate employee** (when someone has left; their requests and documents are kept) and **Reactivate employee**. **Import a list** pastes rows from a spreadsheet, one employee per row, and saves nothing until every row is correct. Identity numbers are shown masked |
| Employee page | One employee: documents, expiry dates, requests, and **Portal access**: **Invite to the portal** sends the employee an email to choose a password. An employee with their own login sees only their own requests and documents, no colleagues and no company invoices |
| **Requests**, **New request** | Every request your company has made. **New request** asks **Who is this request for?** (an employee from your list, or the company itself when the centre has set up a record for it), then the service, the form and the documents; progress is saved as a draft. A request made by an employee with their own login is also a company request and appears here |
| **Approvals** | Quotations waiting for your company. **Approve quotation** issues an invoice to the company and lets the centre continue; **Decline quotation** asks for a reason the centre sees |
| **Documents** | Files your company gave the centre and documents the centre issued |
| **Invoices** | Invoices issued to the company, with **Pay online** when the centre takes online payments, the payments received, **Download receipt**, and the **Statement** (outstanding balance, open balance by age, payment terms, credit limit used; **Print statement**) |
| **Reminders** | Visas, Emirates IDs, labour cards and licences that expire soon, with **Start renewal** |
| **Company profile** | Company details, trade licence, billing (tax registration number), payment terms and contract prices as the centre keeps them (read-only; ask the centre to change them), and **Portal users**: **Invite a colleague** gives somebody the same company-wide access as you |
| **Support** | Questions and complaints to the centre and their answers; **Add a file** to a conversation |
| **Notifications** | Messages about the company's requests and invoices, and **How should we tell you?** (channels for your own login) |

**Employees with their own login** see **My requests** (their own requests through the company, without
amounts), their documents, reminders, notifications and support; they cannot see other employees, the
company's invoices or approvals, and cannot approve or pay anything.

The corporate portal was completed last and has had the least checking; no browser test suite exists for
any portal ([../TEST_REPORT.md](../TEST_REPORT.md), section 2.7).

## 17. The mobile app

A mobile app for customers exists as program code only. It has not been built, tested or published, so there
is nothing to install. Use the portal in your phone's browser; it is designed for small screens and can take
a photo of a document with the phone's camera.

## 18. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **We could not find this centre** | Open the link from your centre again |
| **Your email address is not confirmed yet** | Choose **Send a new confirmation link** and open the link in the email |
| The account is locked after wrong passwords | Wait 15 minutes, or use **Forgot your password?** |
| **The file is not what its name says** | The file type does not match its content. Upload a PDF, JPG or PNG |
| **The upload did not arrive completely** | Upload the file again |
| **This file is still in its security check** | Wait a moment and try again |
| **This file was blocked by the security check** | The file cannot be opened. Upload another copy |
| **Someone just took this time** | Choose another appointment time |
| **This quotation has expired** | The centre will send a new one |
| **The payment provider could not be reached** | No money was taken. Try again in a few minutes |
| **The centre cannot take this online at the moment** | Contact the centre |
| A message appears in English although you use Arabic | Some messages from the system are available in English only |

For anything else, use **Support** in the portal or the phone number and email address on the **Contact**
page.
