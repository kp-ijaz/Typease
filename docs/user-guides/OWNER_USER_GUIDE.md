# Owner guide

For the **owner** of a typing centre and for **managers**. It covers setting the centre up and running it:
onboarding, branches, staff and roles, services and workflows, branding and domains, the subscription,
reports, automations, privacy requests and support access by TypEase.

Day-to-day work at the counter (registering customers, requests, documents, quotations, payments) is in the
[staff guide](STAFF_USER_GUIDE.md); everything there applies to owners and managers too.

Menu names and buttons are written as they appear in the staff application in English. Screens marked
**(owner)** are available to the owner role only unless you give the permission to another role. The people,
companies and numbers in the examples are invented.

> **Three things to know before you start.**
>
> 1. TypEase is software for running your centre. It is not connected to any government system, and it
>    cannot make your centre authorised for anything. What your centre may submit, and to whom, is decided by
>    the authorities and by your own licences.
> 2. The privacy screens are tools. Using them does not by itself make your centre compliant with any law.
>    Have your privacy practices, your retention periods and your invoices reviewed by qualified UAE advisers.
> 3. Messaging and online payment work only when a provider is connected, and the connections in this
>    software have not been tested against the real providers. Test each one before you rely on it.

## 1. Registering and setting up your centre

### How do I register my centre?

On the staff application's sign-in page choose **Register your typing centre**. Enter the **Centre name**, a
**Web address** name (lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens; customers see it in links and it cannot be
changed later), your name, email address, mobile number and a password, and accept the terms. Then confirm
your email address with the link you receive.

If the operator of the platform has switched self-registration off, ask them to create your centre.

### What does the set-up ask for?

After the first sign-in you are taken to **Set up your centre**. Everything is saved as you go; you can stop
and continue later on any device.

| Step | What you do | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Verify your account** | Confirm your email address, and your mobile number with a 6-digit code | Email yes; the mobile number depends on the installation |
| **Business information** | Legal name, trade licence number, tax registration number, emirate, contact details, default language | Yes |
| **Choose your plan** | Pick a plan, monthly or yearly; optionally enter a coupon code | Yes |
| **Branding** | Name and contact details as customers see them | No |
| **Services** | **Install starter catalogue** (13 common services with example prices, checklists and forms) or add your own | At least one service |
| **Branches** | A main branch was created for you. Add its address, phone and opening hours | Yes |
| **Invite your team** | Give your staff their own sign-in | No |
| **Review and finish** | **Finish set-up** | Yes |

A centre's first plan starts with a free trial (14 days in the default plans): there is no invoice at that
point. Before the trial ends you receive an invoice, and the plan continues when it is paid.

After **Finish set-up** your centre can take requests. If the operator reviews new centres first, the page
says **Waiting for approval** and the centre opens when it is approved.

**The prices in the starter catalogue are examples.** Review every price, every government-fee estimate and
every checklist before you take the first request. All starter services are set to "Document preparation
only" or "Information only".

## 2. Branches

**Branches** **(owner)**. Each branch has a name in English and Arabic, a short code, an emirate, an address,
phone, email and **Opening hours**. Customers see these when they book or choose where to be served.

- **Add branch** counts towards the number of branches in your plan. When the limit is reached you are told
  to upgrade.
- One branch is the **Head office**. To change it, open another branch and make that one the head office.
- **Deactivate** removes a branch from the branch switcher, new requests and bookings; its history stays. A
  branch with open requests, and the head office, cannot be deactivated.

Opening hours are information for customers. The times that can actually be booked are set per branch under
**Appointments**, **Calendar settings** (section 10).

## 3. Staff and roles

### How do I add a member of staff?

**Staff**, **Invite staff member**. Enter the name and email address, choose the **Role**, and say **Where
does this person work?**: all branches or selected branches. The person receives an invitation link, chooses
a password and can sign in. Until then they are listed as **Invitation pending**; you can **Resend
invitation**.

The number of staff members is limited by your plan.

### How do I change what somebody may do, or stop their access?

Open the person from **Staff**.

- **Role and branches**: change the role, the branches, and **Available for new work** (switch it off while
  the person is on leave, so that new requests are not assigned to them automatically).
- **Service skills**: the services this person handles. Used when requests are assigned automatically.
- **Suspend**: the person is signed out at once and cannot sign in until you **Reactivate**.
- **Remove from centre**: access ends at once; what they did stays in the history. Open requests assigned to
  them must be reassigned first.

You cannot change your own role, and a centre always keeps at least one owner. Name, email, password and
two-step verification belong to the person and are changed by them.

The **Workload** tab shows open requests and tasks per person, and what each completed in the last 30 days.

![The staff list](../brand/screenshots/centre-adm-staff-en-light-desktop.png)

### Which roles are there?

**Roles** **(owner)** lists the built-in roles. They cannot be changed; copy one to make your own.

| Role | In one sentence |
| --- | --- |
| Centre Owner | Full control: staff, services, prices, money, branding, integrations and settings |
| Centre Manager | Runs the assigned branches day to day: staff, requests, schedules, approvals and operational reports |
| Receptionist | Registers customers, starts requests, uploads documents, prepares quotations, takes payments and books appointments |
| Typing Operator | Works on assigned requests: checks documents, prepares applications and records submission references |
| Document Reviewer | Checks documents and extracted data, and approves prepared applications or sends them back |
| Accountant | Invoices, payment reconciliation, credit notes, expenses and financial reports |

What a manager cannot do, compared with the owner: roles, branches, branding, domains, integrations,
subscription, privacy, settings, fee settings, approving refunds, reconciliation, credit notes, and deciding
on TypEase support access.

The portal roles (Customer, Corporate Administrator, Corporate Employee) are given automatically to portal
users and are fixed.

### How do I create my own role?

**Roles**, **New role** (or **Copy to a new role** on a built-in one). Name it and tick what it may do.

- **Nobody can give more access than they have.** You can only grant permissions you hold yourself, and only
  assign roles that have no more permissions than your own.
- People with a changed role get the change the next time they sign in.
- A role that is in use cannot be deleted.
- **Two-step verification is required only for the built-in owner, manager and accountant roles.** A role you
  create is never forced to use it, even if it has the same permissions. Ask the people who hold a powerful
  custom role to switch it on themselves.

The full list of permissions is in [ROLE_PERMISSION_MATRIX.md](../ROLE_PERMISSION_MATRIX.md).

## 4. Services

**Services** shows everything your centre offers, grouped by category. **Add service** asks for the name,
the category, an internal code and your fee. The rest is set on the service's own page, which has six tabs.

| Tab | What you decide |
| --- | --- |
| **Basics** | Names and descriptions in English and Arabic, eligibility guidance, usual processing time, whether the service is **Active**, shown in the **public catalogue**, can be **requested online**, and at which branches it is offered |
| **Compliance** | **What your centre does for this service** (below) |
| **Pricing** | Service fee, estimates of authority fees and third-party charges, branch prices, which parts are taxed, and the payment policy |
| **Required documents** | The checklist the customer sees, in order: mandatory or optional, and whether a valid document already on file may be reused |
| **Form** | The questions asked for this service |
| **Workflow** | Which workflow the service uses, whether **a reviewer must approve before submission**, and which staff group handles it |

### What does "What your centre does for this service" mean?

It is a description of your own part, and it decides which steps a request may take. It is not an
authorisation, and nothing in TypEase states or implies that your centre is approved by a government
authority.

| Choice | Meaning | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Document preparation only** | Your staff prepare and type the paperwork. The customer or their sponsor submits it | Nothing. The right choice for most typing services |
| **Information only** | General information and guidance. Nothing is prepared or submitted | Nothing |
| **Coordination through an authorised partner** | You prepare the case and an authorised partner submits it. The partner is named on the request | A partner record on the **Integrations** page that is switched on |
| **Direct authorised processing** | Your centre submits the case itself, under an authorisation that your centre holds | An authorisation record on the **Integrations** page that TypEase has verified and that is active |

With the first two choices nobody can record a submission for the service: the customer submits. Customers
see the matching sentence on the service page of your portal.

### How do prices work?

- The **Service fee** is your own charge. **Authority fees** and **Third-party charges** are estimates; the
  amounts a customer actually pays are fixed on the quotation or invoice.
- A price is never edited. **Set a new price** closes the current one and starts a new one, so the **Price
  history** shows what applied when. Prices cannot be scheduled for a future date.
- A branch can have its own price (**Add branch price**). Without one it uses the price for all branches.
- **Tax applies to**: the service fee only, every part of the price, or nothing. The rate is your centre's
  tax rate (section 18). Confirm these choices with your accountant.
- **Payment policy**: **Pay in full before processing**, **Deposit first, the rest later** (with a
  percentage), or **Pay on completion**. Whatever you choose, a request cannot be completed while an invoice
  has a balance.
- **What the customer is charged** previews the result.

![Pricing of a service](../brand/screenshots/centre-ops-service-pricing-ar-dark-desktop.png)

### How do I build the form of a service?

On the **Form** tab choose **Start a form**. Add steps and fields; field kinds are text, long text, number,
date, email, phone, single choice, multiple choice, checkbox, radio buttons and country. A field can be
required, shown only when an earlier answer has a certain value, filled in from the customer's profile, and
marked sensitive (left out of generated summaries). Use **Preview** to try it. **Publish** when it is ready.

- A published version cannot be changed, because requests keep the exact version that was filled in. To
  change the form, **Edit in a new draft** and publish that. Requests already started keep their version.
- Do not ask for identity numbers in a form field unless you must: form answers are stored as ordinary data,
  not encrypted like the Emirates ID and passport number on the customer record.

Categories and document types are managed with the **Categories** and **Document types** buttons on the
Services page. Categories only group your services; they are not a statement that your centre is authorised
by any authority. For a document type you decide whether it **has an expiry date** and whether **expiry
reminders** are created automatically.

## 5. Workflows

A workflow is the list of steps a request goes through. **Workflows** shows yours; a new centre has three:
**Standard service request** (the centre default), **Walk-in express** and **Pay on completion**. Each
service uses one workflow, or the centre default.

### How do I change a workflow?

Open it and choose **Edit in a new draft**. Add, remove, reorder and edit steps. For each step you can set
who acts, a time limit in hours and the role it is escalated to afterwards, whether customers see the step,
and **When this step applies** (conditions). **Check for problems** tells you what must be fixed. **Publish**
when it is ready.

- **New requests follow the new version. Requests that are already running keep the version they started
  with.** A single running request can be moved by hand from its page (**Move to the current workflow**).
- **Roll back to this version** in the version history publishes a copy of an older version.
- **Make centre default** changes which workflow services without their own use.
- A workflow that a service still uses, and the centre default, cannot be archived.

### What can a workflow not do?

**Workflows cannot remove compliance checks.** Whatever the steps say:

- a service that requires a review cannot be submitted or completed without an approved review, and the
  reviewer cannot be the person who prepared the case;
- a service your centre may not submit is never submitted;
- the payment policy of the service applies;
- required documents must be verified before work starts.

The detail is in [WORKFLOW_DOCUMENTATION.md](../WORKFLOW_DOCUMENTATION.md).

## 6. Branding and your own web address

**Branding** **(owner)** decides how your centre looks to customers on the portal, in emails and on PDF
documents.

- **Whose brand do customers see?** **TypEase-branded** (TypEase logo and colours with your centre's name)
  or **White label: your own brand** (your logo, colours, website title and tagline). White label needs a
  plan that includes it. You can switch at any time; nothing is lost.
- **Name and logo**, **Colours** (three colours; text colours are chosen automatically so that everything
  stays readable, and the preview shows the result in the light and the dark theme), **Contact and email
  sender**, **Invoices, receipts and catalogue** (footers, and whether your service list is public),
  **Different details per branch**.
- **Email sender address**: emails are sent from the TypEase address with your sender name. There is no way
  to verify your own address in the application. To send from your own address, connect your own mail server
  under **Integrations**.
- Check an email and an invoice after changing the primary colour: their headers print white text on that
  colour.
- PDF documents are in English only.

**Domains** **(owner)** shows **Your TypEase address**, which always works, and lets you connect your own
domain on plans that include it. **Add domain**, create the two DNS records shown at your domain provider,
wait, then **Verify**. The security certificate is issued automatically afterwards. Custom domains serve the
customer portal only; the staff application stays on its TypEase address. A domain you add is **Reserved for
your centre until** a date 72 hours later: verify it before then, because afterwards another TypEase centre
may claim it, and an entry that is never verified is removed after 14 days. A verified domain stays yours.
If your plan stops including custom domains, the domain shows **Not active**: it is kept for you, but the
portal does not answer on it until the plan includes the feature again (nothing has to be verified again).

A mobile app under your own name in the app stores is a separate project, not a setting.

Everything about branding, domains and their limits is in [WHITE_LABEL_GUIDE.md](../WHITE_LABEL_GUIDE.md).

## 7. Integrations and authorisation records

**Integrations** **(owner)** holds two different things.

**Connections to outside services**

| Kind | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Payment provider | Online payment of invoices by card. Register the address shown on the page in the provider's dashboard so that TypEase learns about completed payments |
| Email (your own mail server) | Sending your centre's emails from your own address |
| SMS, WhatsApp, push | Messages to customers |
| n8n | Automations that call a webhook |

Enter the settings and the secret values, run the **Connection test** where one is offered, and switch the
connection on. Secret values are stored encrypted and are never shown again. Without a connection of your
own, the platform's provider is used for that channel if the operator has set one up; otherwise nothing is
sent on it.

The connections were written from the providers' public documentation and tested against stand-ins. **Test
each one yourself**, including a real payment and refund in the provider's test mode, before you rely on it.

**Authorisation records**

A record states that your centre is authorised by a named authority (reference, validity dates, who holds the
portal account), or names a partner and your agreement with it. **Add a record**, fill it in, **Submit for
review**. TypEase reviews it; only a verified record unlocks "Direct authorised processing" for your services.

- **This is a record, not a connection.** Nothing connects to a government system.
- **Never enter portal credentials.** No portal user name, password, PIN or one-time code is stored in
  TypEase, and the page refuses text that looks like one.
- Changing the authority or partner, the reference or the validity dates sends the record back for review.
  A record whose validity date has passed is switched off automatically.

## 8. Notifications to customers

**Notification settings** (owner and manager) has four tabs.

- **Channels and events**: which channel is ready (Email, SMS, WhatsApp, In the app, Push) and, per event,
  which channels are used. A channel that is not ready sends nothing. "Test mode: messages are written to the
  server log and not sent" means no provider is set up. Security messages are always sent.
- **Templates**: the wording per event, channel and language. Change it and **Preview** it; **Back to the standard
  wording** undoes your change.
- **Delivery log**: every message with its result. **Send again** retries a failed one.
- **Failures**: counts by channel and event.

A message is not sent when the customer has no address for the channel, switched the channel off, has not
agreed (reminders, marketing, WhatsApp), or when the hourly limit per person or your plan's monthly allowance
is reached. The log says which.

WhatsApp needs message templates approved in your WhatsApp business account; TypEase cannot approve them.

## 9. Subscription and plan

**Subscription** **(owner)** shows **Your plan**, **Usage** against what the plan allows, a **Coupon** field,
the **Plans**, and the invoices TypEase has issued to your centre.

The default plans (the operator of your installation can change plans and prices):

| | Starter | Professional | Business | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price per month / per year (AED) | 149 / 1,490 | 349 / 3,490 | 749 / 7,490 | 1,999 / 19,990 |
| Branches | 1 | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Staff users | 3 | 10 | 30 | Unlimited |
| Requests per month | 200 | 1,000 | 5,000 | Unlimited |
| Document storage | 2 GB | 10 GB | 50 GB | Unlimited |
| AI document pages per month | 0 | 200 | 1,000 | Unlimited |
| Notifications per month | 500 | 3,000 | 15,000 | Unlimited |
| Company accounts | 0 | 10 | 100 | Unlimited |
| Portal for company clients, automation rules, AI assistant, scheduled reports | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Your own branding, your own web address | No | No | Yes | Yes |

"API access" is listed for the Enterprise plan, but nothing in the software is switched on or off by it.

### How is my centre billed?

**TypEase does not store card details and never charges automatically.** You pay each invoice yourself from
**Invoices to pay**, **Pay now**.

1. A renewal invoice is issued about a week before the period ends.
2. If it is not paid when the period ends, you are reminded. Your centre keeps working.
3. After the reminders a grace period starts (seven days by default). The page shows **Pay before**.
4. When the grace period ends unpaid, **your centre is suspended**: staff and customers cannot use TypEase
   until the overdue invoice is paid. Access returns as soon as the payment arrives. Your data is kept.

The owner can always sign in to see and pay invoices, even while the centre is suspended.

### How do I change or cancel my plan?

**Change plan**:

- An **upgrade** creates an invoice for the rest of the current period. The new limits and features unlock
  when it is paid.
- A **downgrade** or a change between monthly and yearly takes effect at the end of the period. A downgrade
  is refused while you use more branches, staff, company accounts or storage than the smaller plan allows.

**Cancel subscription**: you keep full access until the end of the paid period. After that staff and
customers can no longer use TypEase. **Keep my subscription** withdraws the cancellation before that date.

When a limit is reached the application says **Your current plan does not allow this** and links to the
plans. Features you lose with a smaller plan stop working, but nothing is deleted: for example, your logo
and colours return when white label is in your plan again.

## 10. Managing the daily work

### How do requests get to my staff?

Operators only see requests assigned to them, so assignment is what gives them work.

- By hand: open the request, tab **Assignment and review**, **Assign an operator** and, if the service needs
  it, **Assign a reviewer**. The reviewer cannot be the operator of the same request. Changing somebody needs
  a reason, and open tasks move to the new person.
- **Assign automatically** chooses the best available operator: first staff with the service among their
  skills, then staff on shift, then whoever has the least open work.
- To do this for every new request, switch on **Assign new requests automatically** in **Settings**,
  **Preferences**.

A request cannot move from **Ready for processing** to **In preparation** without an operator.

### What can only an owner or manager do on a request?

- **Cancel request**, with a reason. A cancelled request cannot be opened again. **Cancelling does not refund
  anything**: if the centre holds money for the request, the history says that finance has to review a
  refund, and somebody has to start it.
- **Reopen** a completed request, with a reason. It goes back to preparation, and an earlier review approval
  no longer counts.
- **Mark as a duplicate**: cancels the request with a link to the original.
- **Assign** and reassign operators and reviewers.
- Approve a **discount** on a quotation. You cannot approve a discount you asked for yourself, unless you are
  the only person who may approve discounts; that case is recorded in the audit log.

### How do I see what is late?

- The **Dashboard** tiles: **New requests**, **Documents to review**, **Waiting for customer**, **Awaiting
  payment**, **Ready to process**, **In progress**, **Completed today**, **Overdue**.
- A request past its due date, and a workflow step past its time limit, create an escalation task for
  managers under **Tasks**.
- **Staff**, **Workload**, and the reports in section 13.

### How do I set up appointments?

**Appointments**, **Calendar settings**. Per branch, add **Booking hours** for each weekday: from, until, the
**length of one appointment** and **people per time**, optionally for one service only. Add **Closed days**.
The **Booking rules** apply to all branches: whether **guests can book without an account**, how many days
ahead, after how many minutes a missed appointment becomes a no-show, until when customers can change a
booking, and when reminders are sent.

### What about customer records?

Owners and managers can additionally:

- **Show full numbers** of a customer's Emirates ID and passport. Each time, your name and the time are
  written to the customer's history. Show them only when you need them.
- **Merge with another record** when one person was registered twice. Compare the two records, choose which
  one to keep, type the number of the record that is retired and give a reason. **A merge cannot be undone**,
  and nothing is ever merged automatically.
- **Archive customer** and **Restore customer**.
- **Delete** and **Restore** documents (restorable for 30 days), create an expiring **share link** for a
  document, and manage **Reminders** for expiring documents.
- Answer and resolve **Support** tickets, and set the **Response targets**.

Placing a **legal hold** on a document, which prevents its deletion, is for the owner.

## 11. Money: what only you can decide

- **Fee settings** (**Expenses & fees**, **Fee settings**) **(owner)**: how each part of a price is named,
  whether it **counts as** centre revenue or is collected for others, and whether tax is charged on it. The
  service fee is always your revenue. Government fees and third-party charges are "collected for others" by
  default and are then not reported as your revenue. **Check these with your accountant. TypEase does not
  give tax advice.** A change applies to new quotations and invoices only.
- **Tax rate** and **Tax registration number** (**Settings**, **Centre profile**) **(owner)**. With a tax
  registration number your invoices are titled "Tax invoice" and print the number. Your invoices do not print
  your address, the customer's address or a company customer's tax registration number, and are in English
  only. Ask your tax adviser whether that is sufficient for you.
- **Approving refunds** is for the owner and the accountant, and never for the person who requested the
  refund. If you are the only person who may approve, give the permission to a second person: otherwise your
  own refund requests cannot be approved by anybody.
- **Reconciliation** and **credit notes** are for the owner and the accountant.
- TypEase records money; it does not move it. It does not pay authorities, and it does not file tax returns.

## 12. Corporate clients

**Corporate clients** (owner and manager; needs a plan with company accounts) holds the companies you serve.

1. **Add company**. It starts as "awaiting verification".
2. **Verify company**: confirm that you checked the trade licence and who may act for the company, and write
   what you checked. Until then its users cannot create requests in the portal.
3. **Approve credit** if the company may be invoiced on credit: a credit limit and payment terms in days.
   Changing the terms later withdraws the approval until it is given again.
4. Add **Authorised contacts** (they are invited to the corporate portal and see everything of the company),
   **Employees** (one by one or in bulk), **Agreements** and **Contract prices**. A contract price replaces
   only your service fee; government and third-party amounts are never part of it. On an employee's record,
   **Portal login** lets you **Send invitation** so that the employee can sign in to the corporate portal
   and see only their own requests and documents; a request such an employee makes there is a request of the
   company about that employee (contract price, the company's administrators approve and pay).
5. **Invoices and statement** shows what the company owes and how old it is.

A company without approved credit pays each invoice like any customer. With approved credit, requests are
not held back by payment, and an invoice that would exceed the credit limit cannot be issued.

**Suspend company** stops new requests but lets its users see what they owe. **Archive** ends portal access.

What your clients see and do in the corporate portal (employees, requests, approvals, invoices and
statement, reminders, company profile, support, notifications) is described in the
[customer guide](CUSTOMER_USER_GUIDE.md), section 16. Company administrators are notified about requests
raised for their company, including those raised by employees with their own login.

## 13. Reports

**Reports** lists **Financial** reports (daily sales, monthly service revenue, transactions by service,
outstanding payments, refunds, liabilities, corporate revenue, service profitability) and **Operations and
customers** reports (requests by status, staff workload, branch performance, customer growth, expiry
reminders, document processing time, customer satisfaction). Managers see the same reports for their
branches.

- Each report says **How these figures are calculated**. Revenue is your service fees less credit notes,
  excluding tax and the fees collected for others.
- Click a figure to see **the records behind** it.
- **Export** to Excel, PDF or CSV. Every export is recorded in the audit log.
- **Send by email on a schedule** (plan feature "Scheduled reports"): daily, weekly or monthly, at a time in
  your centre's time zone, to staff who are allowed to see that report. A recipient who is deactivated or
  loses access is skipped.

Report headings and exports are in English.

## 14. Automations

**Automations** (owner and manager; plan feature "Automation rules") lets routine follow-up happen by
itself: when something happens, do the next step.

### How do I start?

Choose one of the **Ready-made automations**, for example "Tell the manager when a request becomes overdue"
or "Thank the customer when a request is completed", and **Use this automation**. It is added switched off.
Review it, try a **Dry run** (it shows what the automation would do with the most recent event of its kind,
without doing anything), then switch it on.

### What can an automation do?

**When** one of 15 things happens (a customer was registered, a request was submitted, assigned, changed
status, completed or became overdue, a workflow step passed its time limit, a document was uploaded, verified
or is about to expire, a quotation was approved, a payment was completed, an appointment was booked, the
subscription is about to renew), and your conditions are true, it can:

| Action | Note |
| --- | --- |
| Create a task, create a follow-up, escalate | For a role or a person |
| Assign staff | Only fills an empty seat; never replaces the person who has the case |
| Send a message | To the customer, the operator or a role. Consent, opt-outs and limits apply as for any message |
| Schedule an expiry reminder | Reminders are only sent with the customer's consent |
| Generate a summary document | An internal PDF of the request |
| Move the request | Only the steps that may happen automatically |
| Ask for an internal review | The reviewer still decides |
| Call n8n / a webhook | Needs an n8n connection under Integrations. Only identifiers and status values are sent, never names, identity numbers or documents |

### What can an automation never do?

It cannot move a request past a review, a payment or a compliance check; it cannot approve, complete, cancel
or submit; and one automation cannot set off another. If an action is refused or keeps failing, the run goes
to **Gave up, waiting for you**, you get a notification, and you can retry it after fixing the cause.

## 15. Privacy

**Privacy** **(owner)** has six tabs: **Overview**, **Data requests**, **Retention**, **Consent**,
**Incidents**, **Vendors**. The page itself says it: these are **tools, not legal compliance**.

![The privacy screen](../brand/screenshots/centre-adm-privacy-en-light-desktop.png)

### How do I handle a customer's data request?

Customers ask in the portal; for a request that reached you another way choose **Record a request**. Each
request shows the date by which an answer is planned (30 days by default; you can set another) and is flagged
when overdue.

1. **Start review**. Check who is asking. For a request made at the counter or by phone, verifying the
   person's identity is your procedure; the application does not do it.
2. **Approve** or **Reject**, always with a written note.
3. Carry it out:

| Request | Button | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access, or a copy of the data | **Create the data file** | A file with the customer's profile (including identity numbers), contacts, consents, requests with their form answers, the list of their documents, invoices and payments is saved in the vault. The customer downloads it from the portal. It is deleted after 30 days by the default retention policy |
| Correction | **Mark as corrected** | Nothing automatic. Correct the customer record first, then describe what you changed |
| Deletion | **Erase the data** | See below |

### What does erasing do?

Before you decide, **What erasing would touch** shows the numbers.

- It is **refused while the customer has an open request or an unpaid invoice**. The reason is stored on the
  request, where the customer can read it. Close or cancel the requests and settle or void the invoices, then
  try again.
- Otherwise the customer record is anonymised: name, contact details, identity numbers, notes, tags and
  contacts are removed, also on records that were merged into it. Their documents are deleted without a
  restore period. What they wrote (form answers, instructions, comments, support messages) is removed.
  Reminders are deleted and upcoming appointments cancelled. Their portal access ends.
- **Kept**: invoices, payments, refunds and credit notes, unchanged, linked to a record that no longer says
  who it was; the request numbers, services, statuses and dates; documents under legal hold or with a fixed
  retention date. The application lists what was kept and why.
- In the audit log and the other histories the entries stay, with the person's name and details removed.
- Afterwards the record is shown as **Erased customer** with the notice **Personal data was erased**. It no
  longer appears in lists, searches or pickers unless you filter for erased records, and nothing new can be
  attached to it (no request, invoice, payment, document, appointment or ticket); wherever an invoice or a
  request still points at it, the name reads "Erased customer". A record that had been merged into it is
  blanked as well.
- **It cannot be undone.**
- It does not reach backups, emails and messages already sent, files somebody downloaded, or anything held by
  your payment or messaging providers.

Whether anonymising with these exceptions meets your obligations is a question for your legal adviser.

### What else is on the Privacy screen?

- **Retention**: how long records are kept. **Only document deletion happens automatically**: a document is
  deleted the set number of days after its request was closed. The policies for customers, notifications and
  support tickets are stored but nothing applies them. The starting values (documents five years, data
  copies 30 days) are placeholders chosen by the developers, not legal advice: decide your own periods with
  your advisers.
- **Consent**: an overview of what customers agreed to, per purpose, and the history of one customer.
- **Incidents**: a register for security and privacy incidents: what happened, what data, whether personal
  data is involved, whether notification is required, when the authority and the individuals were notified,
  and how it was resolved. **The application records what you did. It does not detect incidents, alert
  anybody, notify any authority, or tell you whether or when you must notify.**
- **Vendors**: every outside company that receives personal data from your centre: what for, which data, in
  which country. A vendor that processes data outside the UAE cannot be approved without written safeguards.
  The register is a record; it does not block a connection that is missing from it.

Your portal's **Privacy policy** and **Terms of service** pages are a general template with a warning that
they must be reviewed by your advisers. The text cannot be edited in the application. Have the template
reviewed, and ask the operator of your installation to change it if needed.

## 16. TypEase support and access to your data

### How do I contact TypEase?

**Support**, **TypEase support**, **Contact TypEase support**. These are your centre's own questions to the
TypEase team. Your customers' tickets are never shown to TypEase.

### Can TypEase staff see my data?

**Not unless you allow it.** When TypEase support needs to look at something, they ask: why, what they want
to see, and for how long. The people who may decide (the owner by default) receive an email and a
notification, and the request appears under **Settings**, **TypEase support access**.

- **Approve…**: you can allow less than was asked. Untick what is not needed, or shorten the time. The clock
  starts when you approve.
- **Deny**: they see nothing. They can ask again with a better reason.
- Access is **read-only**, limited to what you ticked, and **ends by itself**. Opening documents is a
  separate tick. Anything outside what you ticked, and any change, is refused by the system, not just hidden.
- **Revoke now** ends a running access at once.
- **See what was done in the audit log**: every step under the access is recorded with the label "TypEase
  support".

While an access is running, the page shows **Access running now** with who has access and when it ends.

One exception to read-only: if you allow internal notes, support can add an internal note to a request.

## 17. Audit log

**Audit log** (owner and manager) shows who did what and when: staff, customers, company users, automation
rules, automatic steps and the TypEase team. Filter by area, person and date, open an entry to see **What
changed** (before and after), and **Export CSV**.

Entries are written automatically and cannot be edited or deleted by anyone at your centre, including
owners. Identity numbers, form answers and secret values are not written to the log. The one exception to
"cannot be changed": when a customer's data is erased (section 15), that person's name and details are
removed from the entries about them; the entries themselves stay.
