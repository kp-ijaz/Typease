What TypEase is

One typing centre. One live dataset.

TypEase runs a typing centre end to end on one live dataset. Customers submit requests, upload documents, sign and pay from the portal or the app; client companies manage their employees' visas and renewals from the corporate portal; the counter, typing operators, reviewers and accounts work from the staff app; and the platform console runs every centre, plan and subscription. Everyone signs in to the same system and lands on exactly the screen their role needs.

Inside the staff app

7 modules · one app
01

Requests & board

Request wizard with the service's documents, form and price; drag-and-drop board with time limits per stage; automatic assignment, internal approvals, queue tokens, appointments and tasks. The customer is told about every status change.

02

Customers & documents

Customer and company records with encrypted identity numbers, document checklist per request, OCR, versions and access log, expiry tracking, and bulk import of customers and services from Excel or CSV.

03

WhatsApp inbox & leads

One shared WhatsApp inbox with assignment and internal notes; photos and PDFs saved to the customer's documents; a chat turned into a draft request in one click; CRM leads with follow-ups, won and lost.

04

Finance

Quotations, bilingual English–Arabic VAT tax invoices, government fees passed through at cost from an authority fee catalogue, payments, refunds, cashier shifts with cash count, commission and customer statements.

05

Accounting & payroll

Double-entry books posted automatically from invoices, payments and expenses: profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, VAT return. Monthly payroll with allowances and commission, payslips and the WPS salary file.

06

Renewals & reminders

Expiry reminders for every document, a renewal autopilot that sends offer links with online checkout, and unpaid-invoice reminders — by email, SMS and WhatsApp, on schedules the centre sets.

07

Documents, roles & brand

Ready-made letters and contracts in English and Arabic with e-signature and hand-over; custom roles from the permission catalogue, branches, white-label branding and domains, audit log and privacy requests.

Architecture

One monorepo, shared parts

Four Next.js apps share one UI kit, one set of validation schemas, one permission catalogue and one typed API client; the NestJS API is split into a module per domain.

Tenant isolation

Enforced in the database

PostgreSQL row-level security on every centre's tables, so a query can only ever see its own centre — on top of the permission check on every page and endpoint.

Security

Private by default

Encrypted identity numbers and IBANs, two-step sign-in, lockout and rate limiting, signed file links, malware scanning, an audit log and privacy erasure requests.

White-label

Each centre, its own brand

Logo, colours and custom domain per centre; WhatsApp Cloud API, email, SMS, push and online payments behind drivers; Docker Compose to deploy.
